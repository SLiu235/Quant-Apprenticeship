## A1

An acceptable contract: observation = an eligible issuer-day; population = selected four-issuer archive days with usable prior price and post; cutoff = 09:25 ET with assumed publication+60s availability; target = same-day open/close category at ±25 bps; baseline = same market feature model without text; weighting = equal dates after within-date pairing; primary = public-minus-market NLL; success = prespecified improvement with uncertainty on independent data; exclusions/decision = missing inputs excluded and promotion withheld unless evidence and data validity support it. The target is a market response, not an action.

Do not claim 493 independent trials or all Nasdaq listings. The exact contract can be longer in the project; the exercise checks compression without losing the estimand.
