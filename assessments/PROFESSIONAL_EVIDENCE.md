# Professional evidence review — optional, no automatic promotion

This rubric evaluates submitted work or a requested readiness review. It does not assess the learner on the basis of reference-code execution. It supplements the [self-paced policy](SELF_PACED.md).

| Capability | Developing evidence | Stronger evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Originality | Restates a supplied question | Derives a useful new question and distinguishes it from known explanations |
| Market judgment | Describes correlation or price direction | Connects belief difference, payoff, horizon, catalyst, costs and falsification |
| Data diligence | Loads a vendor file | Audits observability, coverage, receipts, revisions, identifiers, exclusions and permitted use |
| Research design | Runs a split and reports a metric | Commits a protocol, records the search, designs diagnostic controls and preserves uncertainty |
| Model depth | Calls a training library | Derives or inspects the objective, diagnoses training, tests representations and explains failure |
| Portfolio contribution | Reports standalone Sharpe/P&L | Measures incremental exposures, turnover, liquidity, capacity and stressed contribution |
| AI research practice | Accepts plausible code or citations | Validates generated work, measures quality/cost and traces failures to source, model or workflow |
| Engineering ownership | Passes happy-path tests | Finds failure causes, recovers state, reconciles economics and explains limits |
| Communication | Summarizes results | Gives a reproducible handoff, survives challenge and changes the recommendation when warranted |

Mark each as unverified, partial, supported or independently challenged. Link evidence. Avoid turning this into a points contest: fabricated evidence, information leakage, invalid accounting or unacknowledged selection invalidates the dependent claim regardless of other strengths.

When review is requested, first identify correct work, questionable reasoning, errors, assumptions and the next investigation. Do not replace the learner's implementation before diagnosis. A separate AI critique is labeled simulated review; it is not independent replication. A real external handoff requires an actual reviewer and appropriate sharing authorization.

Readiness to begin a prospective paper study requires a frozen protocol and trustworthy accounting/recovery for that study's scope. Readiness to allocate capital is a separate investment and operational decision. No module or reference result automatically authorizes it.
