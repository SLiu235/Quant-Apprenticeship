# Project 8 — Deliver and defend an independent research decision

Lessons: 22–24. Integration effort after lessons: 20–35 hours. Workspace connection: Combined portfolio.

## Choose one track

**Response forecasting:** a prospectively acquired, time-valid public-signal/price panel with a frozen baseline comparison.

**Execution/behavior:** order or internal execution logs adequate for the specific fill/policy/utility claim, with observed-state and support audits.

**Feasibility/replication:** a rigorous independent reproduction, sensitivity study or acquisition-design decision when fresh/access-appropriate data is unavailable. This track can pass without claiming fresh empirical alpha.

## Build it step by step

1. Choose one mechanism and complete the contract, source audit, candidate ledger and resource budget.
2. Preflight the whole analysis on labeled fixtures before accessing fresh evaluation outcomes.
3. Freeze selection/retraining/metrics and acquisition rules; distinguish genuine prospective freeze from a retrospective description.
4. Execute the study or document the feasibility limit, preserving all failures and deviations.
5. Reproduce from a clean process and perform a concrete timing/population/statistical/economic review. Resolve correctness defects and rerun affected checks.
6. Deliver an 800-word memo, eight-slide text storyboard, reproducible package and ten-minute defense.

## Complete worked sample

The runnable reference assembles a negative-result committee decision from the independent real pilot audit. Read the companion [worked capstone memo](08_worked_memo.md). The packet's question, estimand, primary interval, no-promotion decision, missing economic/actor evidence and next experiment are explicit. The historical protocol is available in BehaviorIRL; the course packet is a retrospective independent audit, not a newly preregistered discovery.

Its decision is to withhold promotion of the frozen text feature because the primary paired forecast result is unfavorable, while the data also limit broader inference. The next candidate experiment is a prospectively logged panel with fixed sampling, receipts and versions. It is conditional on acquisition value and feasibility; it is not a promise that another model will succeed.

## Student deliverables

Protocol and deviations, source manifest, experiment ledger, code/environment/tests, predictions or feasibility evidence, uncertainty/economic analysis, review/response table, final memo, storyboard and portfolio index. Any inaccessible data must have a documented access path and a permissible fixture; do not distribute data beyond its license.

## Acceptance and answer guidance

Use the graduation rubric. Full credit requires a defensible estimand, valid clocks/population, comparable baselines, honest search/evaluation status, reproducible evidence and a justified decision. Profitability is not a pass condition. Unsupported participant, causal, current-social or extended-session claims fail their respective evidence gates.

Keys 22–24 give protocol examples, review arithmetic and defense answers. For an open-ended capstone there is no required market result: grade design/correctness/interpretation. A correct stop recommendation can outperform a favorable but underidentified backtest.

Final extension: choose the next specialist track only after this defense. Options include exchange execution, cross-sectional asset pricing, causal information research, probabilistic time-series modeling or research infrastructure. List the missing knowledge and one concrete project to acquire it rather than claiming mastery of every quant role.
