## Deliberate boundaries

Required work uses existing local data and explicit synthetic fixtures. API-model, GPU, licensed-feed and actual participant-log extensions were not executed or claimed. The selected real-data examples are teaching/development evidence. Self-assessment and simulated editorial criticism are not independent external certification. The curriculum builds a strong documented core; deeper asset-class, exchange-production and causal-identification expertise needs subsequent specialist projects.
