# Research studio — Independent ownership and prospective paper research

[Professional track](../PROFESSIONAL_TRACK.md) · [Evidence rubric](../assessments/PROFESSIONAL_EVIDENCE.md)

## Objective and prerequisites

Own an original research study through skeptical review, reproducible handoff, operational failure and an explicit investment decision.

Enter from Modules 9–10. This is self-paced project work. You are not being assessed by reading it.

## Core knowledge

Professional ownership combines intellectual and operational accountability. A reviewer needs a contract, data/compute requirements, run command, manifest, results and limitations. Distinguish author reproduction, AI-simulated critique and actual independent replication. Only the last involves a separate reviewer independently executing or verifying the work.

A prospective shadow protocol fixes the decision procedure, model/artifact versions, observation schedule, permissible retraining, metrics, costs and stopping/review rules before outcomes arrive. It must distinguish operational halts from investment drawdown or ordinary forecast noise. Predictions need durable timestamps and no silent editing. The new local registry is useful bookkeeping; a trusted timestamp or independent custodian may be needed for stronger evidentiary claims.

Update beliefs and decide where research effort belongs. Postmortems should identify why the earliest invariant or economic assumption failed, not merely the visible exception. A senior researcher can recommend abandoning a sophisticated system when the expected value of further work is low.

## Mental model

A system owner can explain both why capital should be allocated and why the system can be trusted within its stated scope. A negative investment decision can be the most valuable output.

## Worked case

A shadow system shows deteriorating performance while its data/feature versions reconcile. Do not treat that as proof of an engineering outage. Conversely, a strong P&L run with missing events cannot justify continuing. Diagnosis and investment judgment need different evidence.

## Implementation project

Assemble your original project into a reviewer handoff, recover from an injected failure, and write a committee decision with a counter-thesis. Design a prospective paper protocol using genuinely new observations when available. Do not fabricate a prospective history or schedule unattended activity merely to complete this exercise.

Save independent work under `submissions/professional/06_research_ownership/`. Before coding, define requirements, data contracts, assumptions, alternatives and failure detection. Use the [research agenda](../templates/professional/research_agenda.md), [experiment protocol](../templates/professional/experiment_protocol.md), [edge/data card](../templates/professional/edge_data_card.md) and [committee memo](../templates/professional/committee_memo.md) where relevant.

## Failure cases

Claiming independence for an AI role-play; promising future results from a backtest; changing a shadow model without a new version; treating a halt as authority to discard later genuine fills; equating package tests with researcher competence.

## Debugging exercise

Crash after a fill is durable but before application acknowledgment, then restart. Verify holdings, cash, open-order obligations and any missing external-state evidence. The supplied fill-only store does not solve the open-order portion; document or implement it.

Preserve symptom, competing hypotheses, discriminating evidence, earliest violated invariant, root cause, fix and regression test. Ask for progressively stronger hints if stuck.

## Decision exercise

Choose reject, continue research, or a bounded prospective shadow study. Present the strongest argument against your choice and the evidence that would reverse it. No option automatically authorizes capital deployment.

Write your decision before consulting the reference or requesting the mentor's tradeoff analysis.

## Completion evidence

An original research contribution; reproducible handoff; correctly labeled independent-review status; incident evidence; a frozen prospective protocol or a well-supported rejection; committee memo; updated skill gaps based on actual learner evidence.

Attach evidence for each claim. Missing evidence remains unverified rather than filled with a confident narrative. A meaningful negative result can meet the research objective. Source-code execution alone cannot.
