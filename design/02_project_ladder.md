# Design round 2 — eight projects with a mentor review

**Editorial simulation.** No real CEO reviewed this material.

## Proposed design

Use the eight workspace projects as a ladder: audit → replay → baseline → text → risk → IRL → optimization → independent study. Provide a good first lesson and brief descriptions of the remaining projects. Have a mentor supply explanations, review student work and decide when to advance.

This design improves empirical discipline and connects the existing repositories. It still depends on the mentor to invent most of the teaching at delivery time.

## Simulated CEO critique

“A project list is a work queue. It is not a self-paced course. What does the student do when a result differs from yours? Where are the sample projects, complete solutions and numerical checks? A graduate will copy the nearest library example if you only name ‘risk calibration.’ Your AI content also stops at sentiment: I need a researcher who can audit retrieval time, evaluate an agent trace, budget model latency and recognize contaminated historical evaluation.”

Specific objections:

- Isolated notebooks encourage one-off success; data lineage and deployment state must connect them.
- The learner may mistake a four-stock selected dataset for the Nasdaq universe.
- Unexplained answer expectations make self-grading impossible.
- GPU access is not a prerequisite for good research; more hardware must buy a specified uncertainty reduction.
- A mentor should be optional for progress, although external critique remains valuable.

## Revision decision

Expand to 24 written lessons, three per project, with three assessable assignments each and matching solutions. Supply eight runnable references, including an explicit negative-result research package. Introduce numerical derivations and troubleshooting before student work. Use a shared evidence ledger, assessment rubric and repair process. Add modern AI extraction/evaluation, offline RL support checks, model-date leakage, inference budgets, software tests, failure injection and research communication.

Each project now has an offline required core. Additional data and GPU work are optional extensions with prewritten acceptance criteria; buying a feed does not substitute for showing its observability.

Rejected request: replace the whole course with autonomous agents. Agents assist implementation and adversarial review, while the researcher remains responsible for estimands, timing, evidence and conclusions.

The third design will be reviewed against the finished artifacts and actual execution results rather than approved from a table of contents.
