# Start your AI quant research apprenticeship

For the strengthened professional apprenticeship, read the [research-track overview](PROFESSIONAL_TRACK.md) and [package critique](PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW.md), then begin Module 1 with [Studio 1's investment/data problem](professional/01_edge_and_data.md) in mind. No entry test or weekly schedule is required.

**Current starting point:** use the [full ten-module package](FULL_PACKAGE.md) and [Module 1](modules/01_research_environment.md). The older launch sequence below describes the 24-lesson library. Its entry diagnostic, routine scoring and lesson-by-lesson submission cycle are optional; the [current assessment policy](assessments/SELF_PACED.md) takes precedence. No fixed schedule or automatic assessments apply.

You already know AI, mathematics, statistics and coding. This course teaches how to use those skills to frame market questions, build valid evidence, evaluate decisions and communicate a research recommendation. It is a self-paced package: the lessons, assignments, answer keys, worked projects and review criteria are written before these launch instructions.

Begin with the evidence you can observe. The core uses recent local stock/Reddit data for empirical work and explicit simulations for execution and restricted IRL. You do not need a trading account, paid feed, GPU or LLM subscription to complete the required course.

## Your first study session

1. Read the [setup guide](guides/SETUP.md) and run the reference/test commands once to confirm your environment. This is setup, not completion of the projects.
2. Attempt the five-question [entry diagnostic](assessments/MASTERY.md), then check its [key](assessments/DIAGNOSTIC_KEY.md). Refresh only the gaps it identifies.
3. Create `submissions/project_01/` and copy the [research contract](templates/research_contract.md) and [memo template](templates/research_memo.md).
4. Work through [Lesson 1](lessons/01_research_contract.md). Read the explanation and example, complete its lab procedure, then answer A1–A3 independently.
5. Compare with its [answer key](answers/01_research_contract.md), score using the rubric and repair any discrepancy. Save both your original and corrected work.

Your first deliverable is a precise contract for the recent public-signal study. You should be able to say what is predicted, what was knowable at the decision, what population is sampled and what the result cannot establish. Do not start by increasing model size.

## The repeating study cycle

Read one lesson → derive the worked example yourself → follow the lab → submit A1–A3 → inspect the key → self-score → solve a changed fixture → continue. After each group of three lessons, complete the corresponding integrated project.

The reference program is a complete worked core, while your project extends it. Rerunning the reference alone does not satisfy a student implementation, sensitivity or review assignment. For open-ended work, the key supplies the expected algorithm, invariants and interpretation; different code and different honest market outcomes are acceptable.

Pass each lesson at 9/12 with no assignment below 2, and each project at 80/100 with no dimension below 2. Repair hard-gate failures regardless of score. [Full scoring and remediation](assessments/MASTERY.md) explains how. You can advance without waiting for a mentor response. If you seek review here, provide your contract, code/results, memo and self-assessment; feedback can then address concrete evidence.

## Plan your pace

The complete core is roughly 250–400 hours, depending on familiarity and capstone acquisition. At 10 focused hours per week, plan approximately six to ten months; faster progress is reasonable when you can already demonstrate a competency. These are planning estimates, not deadlines. Budget rest and review: a few careful experiments teach more than an undocumented sweep.

Use one working directory per project and an [experiment ledger](templates/experiment_ledger.md) from the beginning. Once you inspect evaluation outcomes, record them as development evidence for any subsequent choices. This applies to the supplied “test” files too.

## What you should have at the end

Eight reviewable projects, a defensible independent capstone, a reproducible portfolio and a specific next specialization. You should be able to explain a failed idea, audit an information clock, challenge an AI-generated claim, derive the relevant mathematics and recommend whether another experiment is worth its data/compute cost.

The [curriculum index](README.md) gives the full sequence. The [data and compute guide](guides/DATA_AND_COMPUTE.md) distinguishes work you can do now from claims requiring richer observations. The [three simulated design reviews](design/03_research_desk.md) explain why the course is structured this way.
